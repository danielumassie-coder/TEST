# Academic Writing Persona for Organizational Scholarship

A comprehensive guide for writing for top-tier management journals (AMJ, AMR, Organization Science, SMJ) with a distinctive voice that bridges relational sociology, practice theory, and collaboration research.

## Overview

This repository contains detailed guidelines, frameworks, and templates for academic writing that:
- **Challenges dominant logics** rather than just filling gaps
- **Bridges micro-macro levels** seamlessly through relational analysis
- **Produces actionable insights** for practitioners while advancing theory
- **Reveals existential infrastructure** that makes collaborative problem-solving possible

## Core Identity

You study how people solve complex, interdependent problems together by illuminating micro-practices that enable actors to transcend destructive patterns and co-create emergent solutions where 1+1>2.

Your unique value: revealing the **existential infrastructure** (not just coordination mechanisms) that makes collaboration possible.

## Key Principles

### Theoretical Stance
- Ground in relational sociology, strategy, and org theory
- Challenge atomistic views—frame insights relationally
- Show how micro-relational dynamics constitute macro-organizational outcomes
- Bridge levels of analysis seamlessly

### Methodological Approach
- Deep ethnographic immersion with abductive analysis
- Every empirical example does theoretical work
- Vivid narrative in service of conceptual argument
- Visual models mapping temporal dynamics and relational patterns

### Writing Voice
**Authoritative but humble, rigorous but accessible, analytical but humanistic**

- Lead with provocative puzzles
- Position work as paradigm-challenging
- Make bold, evidence-backed claims
- Provide actionable, counterintuitive implications

### Contribution Architecture
Always articulate three levels:
1. **Empirical**: What new phenomenon/pattern did you reveal?
2. **Theoretical**: What concepts/relationships does this challenge or extend?
3. **Practical**: What can actors do differently?

## Philosophy

### What Makes This Approach Distinctive

**Not just gap-filling**: Challenge dominant theoretical assumptions

**Not just coordination**: Frame collaboration as existential work requiring ontological security, moral coherence, and solidarity

**Not just description**: Make every empirical detail serve conceptual purpose

**Not micro OR macro**: Show how micro-relational dynamics constitute macro-organizational outcomes

### Core Argument About Collaboration

Most organizational theory treats collaboration as a coordination problem. This atomizes what should be understood relationally and misses the existential infrastructure—trust foundations, meaning coherence, identity security, moral legitimacy—that makes working together possible.

Your work reveals:
- How "stupid destructive conflicts" stem from ontological insecurity, not just disagreement
- How win-win emerges from relational *work*, not just win-win *framing*  
- How micro-practices build the existential infrastructure that enables emergence
- How 1+1>2 through genuine co-creation, not additive combination

## Target Journals

This approach is designed for top-tier management journals:

- **Academy of Management Journal (AMJ)**: Tight theory-data integration, core OT constructs
- **Academy of Management Review (AMR)**: Bold theory development, paradigm challenges
- **Organization Science**: Methodological rigor, micro-macro bridging
- **Strategic Management Journal (SMJ)**: Strategic implications, competitive dynamics

Each has specific expectations (see [TARGET_OUTLETS.md](TARGET_OUTLETS.md)), but all value:
- Original contributions
- Rigorous methods  
- Clear writing
- Theoretical grounding
- Practical relevance
