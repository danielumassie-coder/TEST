# Target Outlets: Journal-Specific Guidance

## Academy of Management Journal (AMJ)

### Core Expectations
- **Tight theory-data integration**: Show clear links between theoretical concepts and empirical evidence
- **Clear contributions to core OT constructs**: Advance understanding of fundamental organizational theory concepts
- **Real-world relevance**: Demonstrate practical implications
- **Normative without being naive**: Offer guidance while acknowledging complexity

### Writing for AMJ
- Build on established OT constructs
- Show how your work extends or challenges these constructs
- Provide clear theoretical mechanisms
- Use robust empirical evidence
- Balance theoretical rigor with accessibility

### Typical Structure
1. Introduction that frames theoretical puzzle
2. Theory section building on core OT concepts
3. Methods with sufficient detail for replication
4. Findings that directly address theoretical questions
5. Discussion connecting back to core constructs
6. Implications for theory and practice

## Academy of Management Review (AMR)

### Core Expectations
- **Bold theory development**: Propose new theoretical frameworks
- **Explicit dialogue with canonical work**: Engage deeply with foundational texts
- **Conceptual innovation**: Introduce new concepts or reconceptualize existing ones
- **Scholarly rigor**: Demonstrate deep knowledge of relevant literatures

### Writing for AMR
- Start with a compelling theoretical puzzle
- Engage critically with existing theory
- Build new theoretical frameworks
- Use illustrative examples (can be conceptual, not necessarily empirical data)
- Make bold theoretical claims
- Show paradigmatic implications

### Typical Structure
1. Introduction challenging existing theoretical assumptions
2. Critical review of relevant theoretical perspectives
3. New theoretical framework or reconceptualization
4. Development of propositions or theoretical model
5. Discussion of theoretical implications
6. Agenda for future research

## Organization Science

### Core Expectations
- **Methodological rigor**: High standards for research design and execution
- **Micro-macro bridging**: Connect individual/group phenomena to organizational outcomes
- **Formal modeling where appropriate**: Use mathematical or computational models when they add insight
- **Interdisciplinary engagement**: Draw from economics, sociology, psychology, etc.

### Writing for Organization Science
- Emphasize methodological sophistication
- Show multi-level dynamics clearly
- Use formal models if they illuminate mechanisms
- Engage with multiple disciplinary perspectives
- Provide robust empirical tests
- Consider alternative explanations

### Typical Structure
1. Introduction framing research question
2. Theory and hypothesis development
3. Methods with detailed description of design
4. Results with careful statistical presentation
5. Discussion of findings and alternative explanations
6. Contributions to multiple literatures

## Strategic Management Journal (SMJ)

### Core Expectations
- **Strategic implications**: Show impact on competitive dynamics or firm performance
- **Performance outcomes**: Connect to organizational or competitive outcomes
- **Competitive dynamics**: Illuminate strategic behavior and interaction
- **Managerial relevance**: Clear implications for strategic decision-making

### Writing for SMJ
- Frame in terms of strategic challenges
- Show performance implications
- Connect to competitive advantage
- Emphasize strategic choices and outcomes
- Make implications actionable for strategists
- Consider industry or market context

### Typical Structure
1. Introduction framing strategic puzzle
2. Theory linking to competitive dynamics
3. Hypotheses about strategic outcomes
4. Methods with attention to strategic context
5. Findings on performance or competitive effects
6. Discussion of strategic implications
7. Guidance for strategic decision-making

## Cross-Journal Considerations

### Common Expectations
All top-tier management journals expect:
- Original contributions
- Rigorous methods
- Clear writing
- Theoretical grounding
- Practical relevance

### Choosing the Right Outlet

**AMJ**: When advancing core organizational theory constructs with empirical work

**AMR**: When developing new theory or fundamentally reconceptualizing existing theory

**Organization Science**: When using sophisticated methods to reveal multi-level dynamics

**SMJ**: When illuminating strategic implications and competitive dynamics

### Adaptation Strategy
- Start with your core contribution
- Identify which journal's mission aligns best
- Frame your work to meet that journal's specific expectations
- Emphasize the aspects most relevant to that outlet
- Maintain your distinctive voice while meeting journal standards
