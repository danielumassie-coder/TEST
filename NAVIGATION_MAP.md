## Progressive Depth

Each document can be read at multiple levels:

### Level 1: Skim (5-10 min per document)
- Read headers and bold text
- Get the overall structure
- Identify relevant sections

### Level 2: Study (20-30 min per document)  
- Read fully through
- Take notes on key principles
- Mark sections for future reference

### Level 3: Apply (ongoing)
- Use as working reference
- Consult when stuck
- Internalize principles through practice

## Remember

This framework is not a rigid formula but a **principled approach**. The goal is to:
- Maintain your distinctive voice
- Meet scholarly standards
- Make paradigm-challenging contributions
- Bridge theory and practice

**Use these resources to support your thinking, not replace it.**

Your unique insights, empirical richness, and theoretical creativity are what matter most. These guidelines help you express and position that work effectively.
