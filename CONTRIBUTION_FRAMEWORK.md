# Contribution Architecture Framework

## Three Levels of Contribution

Always make three levels of contribution explicit in your work:

### 1. Empirical Contribution
**What new phenomenon or pattern did you reveal?**

- What have we not seen or understood before?
- What empirical puzzle does your study solve?
- What new empirical patterns emerge from your data?

#### Key Questions
- What is novel about what you observed?
- How does this challenge existing empirical understanding?
- What can we now see that we couldn't see before?

### 2. Theoretical Contribution
**What concepts or relationships does this challenge or extend?**

- Which existing theories does your work challenge?
- What new conceptual relationships do you reveal?
- How do you extend or reconceptualize existing constructs?

#### Key Questions
- What theoretical assumptions are you challenging?
- What new concepts or relationships are you proposing?
- How does this change our theoretical understanding?

### 3. Practical Contribution
**What can actors do differently based on this?**

- What practical frameworks emerge from your findings?
- How can practitioners apply these insights?
- What different actions become possible?

#### Key Questions
- What should managers/leaders/practitioners do differently?
- What new capabilities or practices are needed?
- How is this actionable across different contexts?

## Articulating Contributions

### Avoid
- Apologetic framing of contributions
- Separating theory from empirics artificially
- Claiming contributions that the data don't support
- Generic implications

### Embrace
- Clear, bold claims about what you've shown
- Integration of theoretical and empirical insights
- Specific, actionable frameworks
- Transportable insights across contexts

## Making Contributions Land

### In the Introduction
- Preview all three levels of contribution
- Show why each matters
- Create anticipation for what's coming

### Throughout the Paper
- Remind readers of the contribution being built
- Show how each section advances the contribution
- Build cumulatively toward your claims

### In the Discussion
- Deliver on promises made in introduction
- Show how evidence supports each contribution
- Connect contributions to broader conversations

### In the Conclusion
- Synthesize all three levels
- Show how they reinforce each other
- Leave readers with clear takeaways
